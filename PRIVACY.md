# TrueCost — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 14, 2026

TrueCost is designed to collect no data at all.

- **No data collection.** TrueCost does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal
  data, browsing data, or usage analytics. There are no servers, no telemetry, no accounts.
- **Local storage only.** The only data TrueCost stores are the plan numbers you enter in
  the popup (subscription cost and credits included per service). They are saved with your
  browser's extension storage (`storage.sync`), which may sync them across your own
  browsers if you are signed into your browser profile. They never reach us — we couldn't
  read them if we wanted to.
- **Page access.** The content script runs only on the supported sites
  (magnific.ai, magnific.com, freepik.com, higgsfield.ai) and only to read credit numbers
  already visible on the page and draw price labels next to them. It does not read personal
  information, does not modify site data, and does not make any network requests.
- **No third parties.** TrueCost includes no third-party code, SDKs, fonts, or trackers.

Questions: open an issue on the repository.
